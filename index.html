A good session checklist

  • Give your eyes 20 minutes — no screens, no white light.
  • Use red light only to check charts.
  • Check the Moon phase — new Moon is best for galaxies.
  • Let optics cool down for about 20 minutes.
  • Start with a bright anchor, then star-hop.
